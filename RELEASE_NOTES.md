The size estimate now follows what compression really does, especially for scans.

## What's new in 1.0.6

- **Much better estimates for scans** – 1.0.5 compressed many scans far more than its preview showed. A six-page phone scan was shown as 15 % in Smart and really saved 90 %; the preview now says 87 %.
- **Also covered** – the estimate now knows about JPEGs that are re-encoded at their original size, JPEGs that are only repacked, black-and-white scans and very large photos.
- Compression results do not change in this version, only the preview before the run.

## Notes

- The estimate is still a prediction. Plain text documents and some unusual files can differ noticeably from the final size.
- macOS 26, Apple silicon.
- Not signed with an Apple Developer ID: on first launch, click “Open Anyway” in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
- Installed versions from 1.0.2 on offer this update themselves.
